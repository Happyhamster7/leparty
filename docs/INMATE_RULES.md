# Shifty Shen's Slammer — Inmate Rules

_Exported from the shared doc on 2026-10-26. Edit the shared doc first, then re-export._

You're an inmate for the night. Your block competes in games for commissary credits, and spends them on drinks and dirty tricks for the other blocks.

## Getting booked

Scan the QR code on the TV and type your name. You get an inmate number, a charge and a mugshot.

- Everyone starts in the **Holding Cell**. When the hosts build the blocks, you're moved onto one automatically.
- Keep the tab open all night. Your phone remembers you, so closing it is fine but don't clear your browser.
- Arrive late and you stay in the Holding Cell until a host moves you onto a block.

## Your phone

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Now | Your home screen: drinks you owe, punishments to serve, requests waiting on you, then your matchup, the game on now and the gossip feed |
| My Block | Block name and motto, rank, credits, your matchup, purchase requests to second, blockmates, account activity, stash and orders (shows the Holding Cell if that's where you are) |

| Rap Sheet | Your mugshot and the current game briefing |

A red dot on a tab means something needs you: a new briefing, a purchase request to second, or a punishment aimed at your block.

## Blocks and credits

You play in a block of 4 or 5 (Block A, Block B and so on). Each block starts with 20 credits and shares one budget.

- **Earning credits:** win games. The two hosts, the Warden and the Commissary Officer, pay out after every heat and can hand out bonuses or fines. Nobody else can create credits.
- **Spending is a block decision:** pick something in the Commissary and it goes to your block as a request. It goes through when your Shot Caller approves it or 2 blockmates second it. You can withdraw your own request, and the Shot Caller can veto one.
- **Name your block:** your Shot Caller (or anyone, if your block has none) sets a nickname and motto on the My Block tab. It shows on everyone's phones and the TV.
- **Points win the night:** send drinks to other blocks (or the Holding Cell) from **Send drinks** in the Commissary. Every drink costs credits and scores points for your block: Spirit 3, Mystery Spirit 3, Soju Shot 1, Soju Bottle 9, Wine 4, Mystery Cocktail 4. Sending 5 or 10 at once (or a whole Soju bottle) is cheaper and scores extra. Drinks land on a block, not a person: the block decides who drinks them. Most points at the end wins.
- **The shop opens and closes:** the hosts close the commissary at times during the night. While it's closed, nobody can buy or approve requests.

## The four acts

The night changes game every couple of rounds. When the hosts flip the act, your phone (and the TV) switch to a new look, and a card explains the new rules. Your block keeps its balance; only the money's name changes.

Tonight is **The Long Night**:

- **Act I · Booking Day:** soups (ramen) are money. Cell Tosses let your block raid another and pocket up to 10 soups.
- **Act II · Arkham Asylum:** draw from the Joker's deck for your gang, solve the Riddler's four riddles in your Patient File, and find inmates who share your patron villain.
- **Act III · The Heist:** your crew picks a heist role; unspent euros earn more after every game; win streaks pay.
- **Act IV · The Great Escape:** there's a rat in every block, and every vote is a Lockdown.

(If the hosts run the Game Night lineup instead, the acts are:)

- **Act I · Vice City (GTA):** Stick-Ups let your block rob 10 cash from another (they can deflect it back). Watch your wanted level in the corner.
- **Act II · Wish Upon Teyvat (Genshin Impact):** turn your phone sideways. Wish ×1 or ×10 for your party (every 8th wish without a 5★ is guaranteed one). Do your four Daily Commissions in the Adventurer Handbook for Primogems and Adventure Rank. Find blockmates with your Vision: an Elemental Resonance pays extra.
- **Act III · Convergence (TFT):** your block picks one augment. Unspent gold earns interest after every game, and win or loss streaks pay bonus gold.
- **Act IV · Emergency Meeting (Among Us):** the final act, with an Among Us look.

Each act also turns your phone into that game's screen: a GTA smartphone, the Genshin Paimon Menu, the League client and the Among Us lobby. Acts II–IV are played with your phone sideways.

**Shot Cam:** film your block taking its shots (up to 12 seconds) from the camera in the app, add a caption, and it plays on the TV.

When a punishment hits your block, every phone gets an alert: **Take it** or **Deflect**. A deflected punishment can be deflected again. The Holding Cell can't deflect anything. Punishments have no deflecting tonight: take them like a champ.

## Your block's phone

Each block plays on **one phone**. Scan your block's card (or type its code on the site) and that phone plays for the whole block: picking players, shopping and sending drinks, taking punishments and filming at the bar. If another phone scans the card, it takes over. Keep the card face down.

## Each round

The night runs in **4 acts of 3 rounds**: Act 1 · Round 1, Act 1 · Round 2, Act 1 · Final round, then on to Act 2, all the way to Act 4's final round. Every round works the same way:

1. **Game:** your phone shows who's on your block. Pick the players the game needs within **60 seconds** (anyone on your phone can lock it in). Anyone who played either of the last 2 games sits this one out. Miss the minute and the app picks for you. A **briefing** also pops up with how to play.
2. **Results:** the hosts enter placings and rewards. A place usually pays: 1st $44, 2nd $36, 3rd $30, 4th $25, then $21, $18, $15, $11, but the hosts can award less.
3. **Spending:** the shop opens for **90 seconds**, then closes by itself. **Mystery Box** ($30, from round 2): could be a Reflect, or 1–5 random drinks on your block or someone else's. **Burner Phone** ($45): your sends are anonymous for the rest of the round. **Power-ups start the round after you buy them.** Send drinks (they score when they're served), and maybe buy **Reflect** (bounce this round's drinks back, and take their points), **Eye Spy** (see who's been sending what to whom) or a **Bounty**: stake 5–30 of your points, then send 5 drinks within 3 rounds. Hit it and you get **3×** your stake back (**5×** for the Winner's Bounty, only for the block that just won the game). Miss and the stake is gone, plus 3 Spirit shots next round.
**The standings on the TV only update at the end of each act.** Your own block's points on your phone are always live.

4. **Drinks:** blocks go to the bar one at a time, fewest drinks first. Your phone tells you your place in line. When you're up, you have **30 seconds** to finish your drinks. Film it for the TV. Run out of time and it's an extra shot next round. **Finish in time and you earn cash:** it starts at $0 and goes up $5 every round you finish in time ($5, $10, $15…). Run out of time and it drops back to $0.

## The Holding Cell

Late arrivals and anyone kicked off a block wait here.

- **Penalty drinks:** 1 per round.
- **Rules:** play every game with your weak hand; call everyone on a block "boss". Games can add a tougher Holding Cell rule on top; it shows in the briefing.
- **Shopping:** no account.
- **Punishable:** by any block, and you can't deflect.
- **Way out:** a host moves you onto a block.

## Penalty drinks and house rules

**Any drink can be swapped for a water or a sip, no questions asked.** Look after each other.

- Drinks and punishments you owe are listed on your **Now** tab. Tap **Drank one** or **Done** as you serve them. It's on your honor, and the whole room can see the drink tab on the TV.
- The hosts can sentence anyone, any time: a shot, a dare, or both.
- The hosts have the final word on every rule and dispute.
