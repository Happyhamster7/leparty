# Shifty Shen Slammer — Inmate Rules

_Exported from the shared doc on 2026-10-26. Edit the shared doc first, then re-export._

You're an inmate for the night. Your block competes in games for commissary credits, spends them on drinks and dirty tricks, and tries to find out whether a snitch is hiding among you.

## Getting booked

Scan the QR code on the TV and type your name. You get an inmate number, a charge and a mugshot.

- Everyone starts in the **Holding Cell**. When the hosts build the blocks, you're moved onto one automatically.
- Keep the tab open all night. Your phone remembers you, so closing it is fine but don't clear your browser.
- Arrive late and you stay in the Holding Cell until a block bails you out or a host moves you.

## Your phone

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Now | Your home screen: drinks you owe, punishments to serve, votes and requests waiting on you, then your matchup, the game on now and the gossip feed |
| My Block | Block name and motto, rank, credits, your matchup, purchase requests to second, blockmates, account activity, stash and orders (shows Holding or Solitary if that's where you are) |
| Commissary | Spend your block's credits, or ask your block to |
| Rap Sheet | Your mugshot, your secret role (tap to reveal), the current game briefing, and Lights Out and vote screens |
| Board | Block standings, the full roster and the drink tab |

A red dot on a tab means something needs you: a new briefing, a vote, a purchase request to second, or a punishment aimed at your block.

## Blocks and credits

You play in a block of 4 or 5 (Block A, Block B and so on). Each block starts with 20 credits and shares one budget.

- **Earning credits:** win games. The two hosts, the Warden and the Commissary Officer, pay out after every heat and can hand out bonuses or fines. Nobody else can create credits.
- **Spending is a block decision:** pick something in the Commissary and it goes to your block as a request. It goes through when your Shot Caller approves it or 2 blockmates second it. You can withdraw your own request, and the Shot Caller can veto one.
- **Name your block:** your Shot Caller (or anyone, if your block has none) sets a nickname and motto on the My Block tab. It shows on everyone's phones and the TV.
- **Standings:** blocks are ranked by credits earned tonight, so spending doesn't cost you rank.

## The four acts

The night changes game every couple of rounds. When the hosts flip the act, your phone (and the TV) switch to a new look, and a card explains the new rules. Your block keeps its balance; only the money's name changes.

- **Act I · Vice City (GTA):** Stick-Ups let your block rob 10 cash from another (they can deflect it back). Watch your wanted level in the corner.
- **Act II · Wish Upon Teyvat (Genshin Impact):** turn your phone sideways. Wish ×1 or ×10 for your party (every 8th wish without a 5★ is guaranteed one). Do your four Daily Commissions in the Adventurer Handbook for Primogems and Adventure Rank. Find blockmates with your Vision: an Elemental Resonance pays extra.
- **Act III · Convergence (TFT):** your block picks one augment. Unspent gold earns interest after every game, and win or loss streaks pay bonus gold.
- **Act IV · Emergency Meeting (Among Us):** snitches are Impostors, and the stakes double. Every Block Vote is an Emergency Meeting.

Each act also turns your phone into that game's screen: a GTA smartphone, the Genshin Paimon Menu, the League client and the Among Us lobby. Acts II–IV are played with your phone sideways.

**Shot Cam:** film your block taking its shots (up to 12 seconds) from the camera in the app, add a caption, and it plays on the TV.

## The commissary

| Item | Credits | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mixers and ice | 4–10 | Lime and soda, Red Bull, juice or ice, delivered to your block |
| Send a Round / Buy Them a Shot | 15 / 20 | Drinks for another block or the Holding Cell |
| Shot Penalty | 25 | Everyone in the target block owes 1 penalty drink |
| Chain Gang | 20 | Target block plays the next game linked arm in arm |
| Yard Drills | 12 | 10 push-ups or squats each for the target block |
| Confession Booth | 15 | Target block sings a chorus of your choice |
| Shakedown | 20 | Target block loses 15 credits |
| Bail Bond | 30 | Pull someone out of the Holding Cell onto your block (max 5 per block) |
| Snitch Slip | 30 | Deflect card: bounce a punishment back to whoever sent it |
| Bribe the Guard | 35 | Makes a blockmate immune during a block vote |
| Wiretap | 40 | Tells your block whether it has a snitch right now, not who |

When a punishment hits your block, every phone gets an alert: **Take it** or **Deflect**. A deflected punishment can be deflected again. The Holding Cell can't deflect anything. Other blocks only see that you bought "something suspicious" when you buy a Snitch Slip or a Bribe.

## Each game

When a game starts, a **briefing** pops up on every phone. Read it, then tap **Got it**.

1. The briefing explains how to play, plus any extra rules for people in the Holding Cell or Solitary.
2. Every briefing has a **Classified** box. Open it privately. If it says "No orders for you this round," play to win.
3. Most games run in **heats**, a few blocks at a time. My Block tells you "You're up," "Up next," or "Sitting this one out," and the TV shows who's playing.
4. The hosts pay out credits after each heat.
5. Each finished game counts as a round: Holding Cell and Solitary inmates owe their penalty drinks, and solitary sentences go down by one.

If the hosts change the rules mid-game, the briefing pops up again.

## Holding Cell and Solitary

|  | Holding Cell | Solitary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who's there | Late arrivals and anyone voted off a block | Rule-breakers, people sent by an anonymous tip, caught snitches |
| Penalty drinks | 1 per round | 2 per round |
| Rules | Play every game with your weak hand; call everyone on a block "boss" | No talking unless spoken to; sit out games unless the hosts say otherwise |
| Shopping | No account | No commissary |
| Votes | No block vote | No votes, no Lights Out pick |
| Punishable | By any block, and you can't deflect | No |
| Way out | A block buys your Bail Bond, or a host moves you | Serve your rounds; you return to your block (or the Holding Cell if you were kicked out) |

Games can add tougher Holding and Solitary rules on top. They show in the briefing.

## The snitches

About half the blocks have a hidden snitch. Nobody knows which ones. Check your secret role on your Rap Sheet, privately.

- **Sabotage:** a snitch's Classified box holds a secret order to wreck their own block's game. If they pull it off, credits vanish from that block, and every blockmate's phone shows an **unexplained withdrawal**. That's your clue.
- **Lights Out:** everyone picks a name on their phone. A snitch's pick gets that person thrown in Solitary for a round by "an anonymous tip." Everyone else's picks become anonymous gossip about who's most suspected.
- **Block votes:** each block votes on its own members, and it takes a majority to kick someone. A Bribe the Guard makes a blockmate immune.
  - Catch the snitch: your block gets 25 credits plus everything they skimmed. They go to Solitary, then the Holding Cell.
  - Kick an innocent: your block loses 10 credits, and they go to the Holding Cell.
- **End of the night:** every snitch is unmasked. A snitch who was never caught gets to hand out 3 penalty drinks, plus 1 for every sabotage they pulled off, and wins a prize.

## Penalty drinks and house rules

**Any drink can be swapped for a water or a sip, no questions asked.** Look after each other.

- Drinks and punishments you owe are listed on your **Now** tab. Tap **Drank one** or **Done** as you serve them. It's on your honor, and the whole room can see the drink tab on the TV.
- The hosts can sentence anyone, any time: a shot, a dare, or both.
- The hosts have the final word on every rule, sabotage and dispute.
- Don't show your secret role or your Classified box to anyone.
